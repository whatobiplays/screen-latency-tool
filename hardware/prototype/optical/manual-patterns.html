Manual optical patterns

slt-manual-optical-patterns v1.0.0

  1. Start fullscreen to establish BLACK (#000000). Wait for browser overlays to disappear before observing the panel.
  2. Tap the left quarter of the page for BLACK or the right quarter for WHITE (#FFFFFF). The middle half is inactive. Use a brief tap; dragging, holding longer than half a second, and multiple touches are rejected.
  3. Hold each requested state for at least 2 seconds. Extend the dwell until you have at least 1 second of settled observation after any overlay or disturbance clears. The page never advances the pattern automatically.
  4. Exit fullscreen with the browser or system gesture to export diagnostics. Fullscreen loss, backgrounding, loss of focus, and viewport changes invalidate the segment. Start a fresh fullscreen setup at BLACK before continuing.

The export records requested colors and browser lifecycle diagnostics. It does not measure luminance or latency. Browser validation does not establish an optical pass. Keep the USB probe session independent.

JavaScript is required for fullscreen setup and diagnostics.

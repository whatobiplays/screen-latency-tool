This note consolidates purchasing evidence for one low-cost, one-sensor Teensy 4.1 prototype bench. It records dated observations and open gates. It does not authorize a purchase or report assembly, energization, USB synchronization, or qualification. The five-channel production fixture remains outside this basket.

The *planned* purchase basis is one routed 50 mm by 44 mm two-layer optical board design and one OSH Park set containing three physical boards: **one to populate** and **two to retain as bare spares**. This is a quantity plan, not evidence that any board has been purchased, received or populated. The proposed bench also uses one Teensy 4.1 and an offboard breadboard power and USB assembly.

The current generated manifest has **44 modeled rows and 34 unique part identities**. The **31-MPN purchasing basket** contains 37 required populated units, one DNP resistor, and six non-purchasable PCB/test-pad rows. All rows have price fields. The generated BOM is **$138.89** and Strategy A is **$138.94**. The owner-approved `KGM15BR71H104KM` replaces `KT` at the same $0.10 unit and $0.30 three-part cost. Adafruit 3878 replaces the earlier 5030+592 stack; the eLabGuy breakout and Fluke leads remain $49.27. The five selected connector lines total $12.56. The current exact-MPN screen is **24 REVIEW / 7 HOLD**. L721A/GST18U05-P1J are conditional REVIEW for owner purchase review; eLabGuy and both AMASS XT30U parts remain HOLD for exact sourceability gaps. REVIEW does not authorize purchase or physical release; supplier stock does not establish manufacturer lifecycle.

The updated Strategy A source snapshot is **$138.94**: $138.20 in non-J2 lines plus the $0.74021 exact-MPN J2 pair (rounded at presentation). Strategy B and the generated BOM total **$138.89**: $138.15 in non-J2 lines plus the same J2 pair, rounded to $0.74 in BOM.csv. These totals combine dated supplier observations. Comet's exact-MPN prices are USD before VAT/shipping; U.S. delivery remains unverified. Shipping, taxes, duties, fabrication/PCBA, complete harnesses, mechanics and equipment remain outside the component subtotals. Neither subtotal is a checkout quote.

The original lifecycle audit covered 23 MPNs and did not establish exact-SKU manufacturer lifecycle status for DEV-20360; PJRC’s manufacturing transition evidence is family-level. The current basket has 31 purchasing MPNs: five exact-MPN lifecycle-confirmed and 26 lifecycle-unverified. Unknown lifecycle is a factual record, not by itself an automatic procurement prohibition for this one-sensor prototype under the revised SPEC-X-001 conditions. Two Samsung C-suffix options have model-level Mass Production evidence but no suffix-specific lifecycle record. The 2023V2 AMASS specification names both selected XT30U .G.Y codes, while the newer 2026V1 catalog adds a UL token without stating equivalence to the selected codes. Catalog presence and seller availability do not establish manufacturer lifecycle. Both J2 entries remain UNVERIFIED_BEFORE_ORDER and HOLD.
